## Folhas de movimento e combate

As 18 folhas foram geradas no ImageGen integrado com o modelo aprovado do próprio alienígena como referência de proporções, uniforme e paleta.

**Movimento, folha ground** · Six columns by three rows, eighteen transparent cels. Row 1 contains six subtly distinct breathing poses. Row 2 is six forward walking poses with contact, recoil, passing, high point, opposite contact and passing. Row 3 is six distinct backward steps with guarded posture, facing right. Draw full separate bodies, floor-aligned, with generous transparent gutters.

**Movimento, folha drive** · Five columns by three rows, fifteen transparent cels. Row 1 contains five forward dash poses from coil to recovery. Row 2 contains five backdash poses facing right from weight shift to recovery. Row 3 contains two crouch startup poses, two crouch idle poses and one crouch exit pose. Change stance with bent joints.

**Movimento, folha air** · Four columns by four rows, sixteen transparent cels. Row 1 contains standing crouch exit and three jump preparation poses. Row 2 contains two rise poses, jump apex and early fall. Row 3 contains late fall and three landing poses. Row 4 contains standing damage, standing guard, crouching guard and a horizontal downed pose.

**Combate** · Four columns with four separate drawings per technique, showing preparation, execution, contact and recovery. Facing right in three-quarter view. Use clean pixel edges, complete silhouettes, transparent gutters and original anatomy. The engine adds the combat effects.

- **Diamante** · Folha 0 com Crystal Jab, Crystal Blade e Low Crystal Strike. Folha 1 com Crystal Sweep, Air Punch e Downward Crystal. Folha 2 com Crystal Throw, Espinhos de Cristal, Muralha de Diamante e Rajada de Fragmentos.
