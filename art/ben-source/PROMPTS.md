# Ben Tennyson — fontes de arte

Atlas de seis cels de Idle e seis cels de normais humanos gerados com ImageGen. O empacotamento conserva o canal alfa do personagem, usa nearest-neighbor e fixa o pivô no chão.

```powershell
node scripts/run_art.mjs scripts/pack_ben.py
```

## Idle

Use case: game-ready raster spritesheet, transparent pixel art. Design BEN TENNYSON as the SLIM TEENAGE BEN from BEN 10 OMNIVERSE, unmistakable at game size. Source visual brief: medium brown tousled angular hair sweeping across forehead, short pointed side locks, clear bright green eyes, expressive cocky half smile, clean youthful angular face, black short sleeve t-shirt with ONE bright green vertical central stripe and small blocky WHITE number 10 centered on it at the sternum, narrow green side stripes, long warm tan-brown cargo pants, classic green and white sneakers, and bulky grey WHITE and black completed Omnitrix with bright green round hourglass face on his LEFT wrist. It is the left arm with the watch; on the near arm, Ben keeps the forearm slightly out in front so the grey watch and green dial are legible. No jacket, no gloves, no cape. Distinct slender teenager build, light relaxed fighter stance with one foot slightly forward and loose knees, both feet planted; one hand ready but open, neck relaxed, lively hair. Body and gaze face screen RIGHT in a clear three-quarter view, with the front of shirt visible. Exactly SIX separately hand-drawn cels, equal 3 columns by 2 rows, 1536 by 1024 canvas, each character wholly inside own cell with generous transparent gutters and boots aligned to the same baseline within each row, 3:4 human silhouette at consistent visual scale, no written labels. Frame sequence: ready, draw breath with shoulders raised subtly, weight drifts forward and watch tilts naturally, weight shifts to back foot with slight relaxation in free hand, settle with shoulder and fringe dropping gently, prepare to return to ready. Small but legible pose changes with constant body proportions, consistent face and outfit. Authentic richly shaded 1990s 2D arcade fighting game PIXEL ART, clearly visible square pixels and selective angular highlights; whole sprites roughly 130 pixels tall when converted to the game's native resolution. No antialias, airbrush, painting, fog, halos, cast shadows, effects, text besides the shirt number. Transparent alpha background. The two local attachments are ONLY references for the game's line weight, sprite scale and crisp rendering. Do not borrow their body shapes or clothes.

## Normais

Use case: game-ready sprite sheet expanding the supplied character asset. Use the provided idle sheet as exact character and pixel-art style reference. Same lean teen Ben Tennyson from Ben 10 Omniverse in every drawing, same head/body ratio, messy brown hair, emerald eyes, black and green 10 shirt, tan-brown cargo pants, green-and-white sneakers, completed gray Omnitrix on the LEFT wrist. Facing right in 3/4 view. Generate 1536×1024, three columns by two rows with huge transparent gutters and fixed character height, full bodies including feet. Top row: one right-handed quick JAB in three poses, slight windup, fully extending right hand forward to chest height with clinched knuckles and left watch hand guarding, then recoiling. Bottom row: medium side KICK in three poses, tucked knee chamber with left watch arm bracing, firm clearly readable right leg strike to the right at waist height on the left planted sneaker, and controlled recovery with bent leg. Hands and face match reference at all times. Exact same line weight, palette and hard deliberate pixel clusters, no photorealism, no softness or rendering outside the sprites. All six drawings are different but coherent. No gradients, floor, effects, drop shadows, action lines, labels or writing beyond white 10 on shirt. Transparent background. Keep figures fully within their equal grid cells.
