# Bellicus e Serena

Arte feita com o ImageGen integrado para a Super Alien X. A prancha foi recortada em dois PNGs RGBA e reduzida com nearest-neighbor. [Bellicus](../../public/sprites/fx/alien-x-bellicus.png) e [Serena](../../public/sprites/fx/alien-x-serena.png) entram como camadas da cena após a confirmação do golpe.

Referência visual [Bellicus em Ben 10](https://ben10.fandom.com/wiki/Bellicus). Referência de cena [So Long, And Thanks For All The Smoothies](https://ben10omniverseref.fandom.com/wiki/OVS1E7_So_Long,_and_Thanks_For_All_the_Smoothies).

## Prompt

A production-ready 16-bit fighting-game pixel art sprite pair for Ben 10 Omniverse, Bellicus and Serena, the two green consciousnesses inside Alien X. Wide landscape sheet on a genuinely transparent background. Exactly two separate giant floating faces: LEFT Bellicus, severe older masculine green face with angular heavy brows, recessed eyes and large ridged jaw, turned slightly inward to screen right. RIGHT Serena, composed feminine jade green face, long graceful cheek planes and a small kind but commanding closed mouth, turned slightly inward to screen left. Both faithful to the Ben 10 animated series appearance of spectral luminous emerald-green masklike disembodied human faces without visible hair, shoulders, neck or bodies. Deep emerald shadows and pale mint highlights with just a few sharp white pixels in the eyes. Clear readable outlines at gameplay scale, M.U.G.E.N arcade cutscene dramatic lighting, dense crisp pixel clusters, no outlines beyond sprite aura, no smooth painterly gradients. Center both in separate halves, clear 15 percent transparent gap, generous clear margins and do not overlap. No background, no stars or stray artifacts, no typography, only these two floating green heads.
