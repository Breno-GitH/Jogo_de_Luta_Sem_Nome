## Frenesi da Kakuja

`frenzy.png` foi gerado no ImageGen integrado com as pranchas de salto e queda como referências. O rosto final e os pontos de montagem são aplicados pelo pipeline de arte.

Use case: game sprite asset, transparent spritesheet. Existing Tokyo Ghoul Ken Kaneki white-haired fighting game model (see the two supplied sprite sheets). Draw exactly SIX body poses for a new speed attack, 3 columns × 2 rows, equal cells with huge transparent padding, 1536×1024. Follow the reference's style closely: clearly defined crisp 2D fighting game pixel art, black crew high-collar short sleeve top, black baggy pants and black boots, lean body, light skin, white shaggy hair, his small black ghoul mask. No kagunes, no effects, no text, no walls, no contact markings, no visible background. The program will supply limbs and scenery. Keep character facing screen RIGHT in all six authored cels. Top row: 1) low forward pounce about to sprint, both boots grounded, body leaning right, arms back; 2) mid-air fast long horizontal bound with torso near horizontal, both hands back, one knee pulled in, foot pointed; 3) airborne wall planting with BOTH boot soles at same X at LEFT edge of his sprite (feet pointing left, knees bent toward chest), facing right away from imagined left wall, torso leaned right, right arm coiled. Bottom row: 4) explosive diagonal down-right wall push-off, leftmost leg fully stretched back toward imaginary wall on left, right knee bent, leading hand reaches down right; 5) landing on one knee after a heavy attack, one hand touches floor, head low, one knee up; 6) the same low stance slowly lifting head and opening shoulders with a predatory sideways stare, grounded feet and one hand still near floor, face lit by tiny red eye. SIX consistent poses, same face and proportions, different explicit silhouettes, clean complete body per cell with no overlap. Strong compact recognizable knee and shoulder anatomy, do not add anything to black outfit. Hard square pixels.
